Nine matches into the 2026-27 Premier League season, the most surprising name in the top four is Brighton. The Seagulls sit third on 19 points, and their goal difference of plus seventeen is the best in the entire division.

## The win that announced them

Brighton 1-0 win at Manchester City on Saturday was the result of the weekend. City had been level with Arsenal at the top before the match; by Saturday evening they were three points behind the leaders, and Brighton had announced themselves as genuine top-four contenders rather than early-season tourists.

## Why the numbers demand respect

A plus-seventeen goal difference after nine matches is not an accident. It means Brighton are not just grinding out narrow wins - they are controlling matches, scoring freely and defending well at the same time. Teams that combine all three tend to stay near the top far longer than anyone expects.

## The chasing pack behind them

Behind the top three, Chelsea sit fourth on 16 points and Liverpool fifth on 15, with Brentford on 14 and a cluster of Bournemouth, Tottenham and Aston Villa on 12. The table is congested, which makes Brighton position all the more valuable: they already have breathing room over the clubs chasing the Champions League places.

## The question now

The question for Brighton is the same one every surprise package faces: can they sustain it when the fixtures get harder and the squad gets tested? On the evidence of the first nine matches, nobody should be betting against them.
