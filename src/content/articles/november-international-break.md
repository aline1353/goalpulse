Gameweek 10 finishes on Sunday 8 November with Manchester United against Aston Villa, and then the Premier League goes quiet. There is no league football at all until Saturday 21 November, when Gameweek 11 begins with Manchester City against Fulham. That is 13 days without a Premier League match, the longest pause of the season so far.

## Why the league stops

The break is for international football. National teams play their fixtures in this window, and clubs have to release their players. It is the same pattern every season: the league builds its calendar around a handful of international windows, and November is one of them. With players scattered across the world, there is no way to play league matches at the same time.

## What happens during the break

For the players who are not called up, it is a rare chance to rest and train. For the ones who do travel, it is two more high-intensity games, often with long flights in between. That is why the break matters so much to FPL managers: a player can come back fit and in form, or come back carrying a knock picked up thousands of miles from home. Team news in the days before Gameweek 11 will matter more than usual.

## The run-up to the pause

Before the break, Gameweek 10 runs from Friday 6 November to Sunday 8 November. Everton host Coventry City on Friday night, Saturday brings Leeds against Tottenham, Arsenal against Hull City, Fulham against Newcastle and Nottingham Forest against Manchester City, and Sunday has Brighton against Brentford, Crystal Palace against Liverpool, Ipswich against Bournemouth, Sunderland against Chelsea and Manchester United against Aston Villa. It is the last chance for teams to build momentum before the pause.

## What it means for the season

Breaks like this can change a season. A team in form loses its rhythm, a team in trouble gets two weeks on the training ground to fix things, and injuries picked up on international duty can force managers into changes they never planned. Arsenal go into the break top of the league, and they will want to come back the same way. Everyone else will hope the pause is the reset they need.
